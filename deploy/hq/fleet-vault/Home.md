# CoreCuity fleet vault

What the fleet knows that its tools cannot fetch. Plain Markdown; open this folder in Obsidian to browse it.

## Two kinds of note

| | Written by | Can the fleet change it? |
|---|---|---|
| `clients/<client>/card.md` — what the client is, what each system is, the founders' standing instructions, who the contact is | a founder, shipped from the Mac | no |
| `clients/<client>/timeline.md` — the recent chronology the vault started from | a founder, shipped from the Mac | no |
| `clients/<client>/journal/YYYY-MM.md` — what the fleet recorded since, each entry dated and with its source | the fleet, through `fleet-note` | append only |
| `fleet/journal/YYYY-MM.md` — lessons about doing the job | the fleet, through `fleet-note` | append only |

A journal entry is the fleet's own record. When it and a card disagree, the card wins and a founder is told.

## What is never here

Prices, fees and budgets. Email addresses and phone numbers. Anything about a client's customers. Passwords, tokens and where they are kept. Known weaknesses of a server. CoreCuity's ownership, contracts and staff matters. One client's facts in another client's folder.

## Where the rest lives

- Is it up, backups, domains, expiries → Sentinel.
- Where the work stands → Plane.
- Ad numbers → Meta.
- The company's own record → the founders' memory store, which is not on this server.
