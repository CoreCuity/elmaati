## TL;DR

- **What it is:** A Casablanca AI/automation **agency** (hunterbi.com) — Darija WhatsApp chatbots + Odoo integration + n8n automation + VoIP/BI/CRM. Founder-led, **micro-team (~5–15 people)**, bootstrapped, no funding.
- **Real threat = SEO, not scale.** They **dominate the search demand** ("chatbot darija maroc whatsapp", "intégrateur Odoo Maroc") and run a one-stop-shop breadth. That's the genuine competitive pressure.

- **Credibility inflation is significant:** claims "8+ years" but domain is from **Jan 2022**; "official Odoo partner" but **absent from Odoo's directory**; "OpenAI authorized reseller for MENA/Africa", MIT/Stanford/PhD team, US + Germany "offices" (almost certainly virtual addresses) — all unverified or contradicted.

## 7. Battlecard — CoreCuity vs Hunter BI

**Overlap:** Darija WhatsApp chatbot · Odoo integration · n8n automation · Moroccan SMEs · Casablanca-centric.

**Their strengths (respect these):**
1. **SEO dominance** — they own the inbound demand for the exact terms our buyers search.
2. **One-stop breadth** — VoIP + BI + CRM + web dev under one roof.
3. **Brand-longevity narrative** + GITEX visibility.
4. Early **AI-reseller positioning** (Claude/Anthropic, "OpenAI").

**Their weaknesses = our attack vectors:**
1. **Agency, not a product → no scale, high cost.** Their floor is 15k setup + 8k/mo; our ~2,000 MAD/mo + setup is **4–8× cheaper** at entry → we reach the true SME long-tail (artisans, single-location). *"They build custom for mid-market; we're ready-made for the SME that can't afford a 6-month integration."*
2. **No multi-tenant platform / vertical packs → slow.** *"We go live in 48 hours; they quote 8–14 weeks."*
3. **No Darija VOICE agent — genuine whitespace.** *"The only SME platform with a Moroccan voice agent — Elmaati answers your calls in Darija so you don't miss revenue."*
4. **Thin, unverifiable proof.** 1 confirmable client, 11 reviews, fictional-looking case studies. *We win with 3 real, permissioned case studies with live dashboards and real numbers.*
5. **Pricing opacity + credibility inflation.** Scattered prices, "official Odoo partner" they're not. *Published pricing + verifiable partner status = trust for burned SME buyers.*
6. **Generalist, no vertical pre-config.** *"Built for your riad / garage / boutique — not a generic system your team must train."*

**Strategic read:** Hunter BI validates the market (demand for Darija WhatsApp + Odoo AI is real and searched) but is a **small, marketing-heavy, bespoke shop with notable credibility gaps** — not a scalable platform. The one thing to genuinely counter is their **SEO moat**: we need our own content/SEO play for the same terms, plus verifiable proof and the voice + productization wedges they can't match. They're also drifting toward products (BizPlan.ma) and reseller margins — watch whether they productize the chatbot into multi-tenant SaaS; that's the only path that would make them a structural threat.
