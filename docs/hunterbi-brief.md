# Hunter BI — Executive Brief

> Quick-read version. Full detail: [`competitor-hunterbi-dossier.md`](./competitor-hunterbi-dossier.md). Compiled 2026-05-26.

## What Hunter BI actually is
A **Casablanca AI/automation agency** (hunterbi.com) — Darija WhatsApp chatbots + Odoo integration + n8n automation + VoIP/BI/CRM. Founder-led **micro-team (~5–15 people)**, bootstrapped, no funding. Run by **Hamza EL HINANI** (CEO, from near Beni Mellal; oddly maintains *two* LinkedIn profiles) + one sales manager. It's CoreCuity's closest *visible* full-stack competitor — but it's a small, bespoke shop, not a platform.

## The credibility picture (read this part)
A lot of the brand is inflated. Verified vs claimed:
- 🚩 Claims **"8+ years"** — but the domain was registered **January 2022** (and another page says "5 years"). Realistically ~3–4 years old.
- 🚩 Claims **"official/certified Odoo partner"** — but is **absent from Odoo's official Morocco partner directory** (15 partners listed). That's a real flag.
- 🚩 **"OpenAI authorized reseller for MENA & Africa"** — self-issued; OpenAI publishes no such list. Microsoft/Google/AWS "partnerships" similarly self-claimed.
- 🚩 **US (Washington DC) + Germany offices** — addresses map to known **virtual-office clusters** (Eisenhower Ave, Alexandria; a Saarbrücken business center). No registered entities.
- 🚩 "Trained on **10M+ Moroccan dialect conversations**", "MIT/Stanford/PhD team", "375% avg ROI" — no evidence; boilerplate.
- 🚩 **Proof is thin:** exactly **one** independently verifiable client (Palmarosa, Casablanca cosmetics). The rest is a logo wall + likely-fictional case studies ("GreenBuild", "CasaPacking"). **11 Google reviews**, no Clutch/Trustpilot.

## Pricing (their floor is high)
AI automation **15,000 MAD setup + 8,000/mo** (cheapest tier) up to 180k+; Darija WhatsApp chatbot **25k / 59k one-off**. It's **project-first, not SaaS** — and **4–8× more expensive at entry than CoreCuity's ~2,000 MAD/mo.** Inconsistent prices across pages. First actual product: **BizPlan.ma** (Dec 2025, AI business-plan generator — different buyer). They also resell Claude/Anthropic + Odoo licenses.

## The ONE thing to actually respect: their SEO
This is the real competitive pressure. They **rank page 1 for "chatbot darija maroc whatsapp"** and own the French-language Moroccan AI search demand (30+ blog articles, heavy geo-targeting). SEO is their entire acquisition engine — and right now they're capturing the buyers searching for exactly what CoreCuity sells.
